- Gmail and Google Calendar: 2026 SVG icons linked on official [Gmail](https://workspace.google.com/products/gmail/) and [Calendar](https://workspace.google.com/products/calendar/) pages. The page links [brand protection policy](https://kakao.com/policy/brand/trademark?lang=ko). No separate permissive asset license was established. Retain the mark unchanged for service identification.
- GitHub: black Invertocat SVG from the archive directly linked by [GitHub Brand Toolkit / Logo](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo). Its guidance prohibits distortion and misleading endorsement; [logo policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/other-site-policies/github-logo-policy) links to usage rules. Keep GitHub identification clear in the service context.

These observations document provenance and published guidance, not blanket legal clearance. No service was connected and no messages, calendar changes, repository actions, or applications were submitted.
